About

My work spans cfDNA deconvolution for cancer detection, six-letter methylation sequencing analysis and feature extraction, chromatin profiling (CUT&Tag and ChIP-seq), G-quadruplex characterization and detection, DLP+ single-cell copy number analysis, CRISPR screen analysis, and pharmacogenomic drug effect modeling.

I hold an M.Sc. in Bioinformatics from the University of British Columbia (GPA 95.3/100) and a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Amirkabir University of Technology.

Research

Current work at the Aparicio Lab

My research focuses on building machine learning tools and scalable data analysis pipelines to decode large-scale genomic and epigenomic data in the context of cancer biology.

⬡

mod-squares: ctDNA Deconvolution

Developing mod-squares, a framework for cfDNA deconvolution from plasma methylation data. The pipeline spans feature extraction, data cleaning, and statistical and ML-based deconvolution to infer tissue-of-origin and tumor fraction for minimal residual disease detection.

MethylationcfDNAMRDML
◈

CUT&Tag & ChIP-seq Signal Modeling

Building end-to-end pipelines using Enformer and Borzoi transformer architectures for large-scale chromatin profiling and regulatory element characterization from CUT&Tag and ChIP-seq data.

EnformerBorzoiCUT&TagChIP-seq
◇

Biomodal 6-Letter Methylation Sequencing

Designing 5mC/hmC-aware feature extraction methods for Biomodal's six-letter sequencing technology, enabling simultaneous detection of cytosine modifications at single-base resolution in cancer cell lines.

5mC/hmCBiomodalEpigenomics
○

Direct Library Preparation+ Single-Cell WGS

Analyzing DLP+ (Direct Library Preparation+) single-cell whole-genome sequencing data from CX5461-treated cells to identify copy number–based predictors of pharmacological drug response across clonal populations.

DLP+scWGSPharmacogenomics
△

G-Quadruplex Characterization

Developing deep learning models to characterize G-quadruplex regulatory structures and their role in genome stability.

G4Deep LearningRegulatory Genomics
□

CRISPR Screen Analysis

Designing computational workflows to normalize and visualize guide enrichment patterns in single-cell perturbation screens, enabling identification of context-specific genetic vulnerabilities in cancer cells.

CRISPRPerturbationSingle-Cell
